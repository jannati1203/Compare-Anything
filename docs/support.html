About Compare Anything

Compare Anything is a Chrome extension that allows you to explicitly select 2 to 4 webpages and generate an objective, evidence-based side-by-side comparison table without switching between tabs.

How to Use

  1. Open any webpage you want to compare (e.g., product, course, job posting).
  2. Click the Compare Anything icon in your browser toolbar.
  3. Click "Add Current Page".
  4. Repeat for 1 to 3 additional webpages (up to 4 pages total).
  5. (Optional) Type what matters most to you in the priority field.
  6. Click "Compare Pages" to generate your visual comparison table.

Common Questions & Troubleshooting

  • Page extraction limitations: Compare Anything extracts structured specifications, tables, lists, and headings. Very heavy dynamic canvas apps, PDF viewers, or pages requiring manual interaction to display specifications may yield limited text.
  • Why does a spec say "Not stated"? Our AI operates strictly on evidence. If a page does not explicitly state a specification, we mark it as "Not stated" instead of guessing or hallucinating facts.
  • Why does it say "No clear winner"? When the facts on the compared pages are tied, inconclusive, or subjective, Compare Anything provides a balanced explanation rather than forcing an arbitrary winner.
  • Service availability & rate limits: To ensure fast, free, and reliable comparisons for all users, rate limits are enforced (10 comparisons per day per installation, 30 per IP per hour).

Privacy & Security

Compare Anything accesses pages only when you explicitly click "Add Current Page". It never scans your history or unselected tabs. No user accounts or personal data are collected.
